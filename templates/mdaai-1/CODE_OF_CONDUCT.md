# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a professional, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Expected behavior includes:

- Communicating respectfully and constructively.
- Focusing critique on work, evidence, and outcomes.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes and correcting them.
- Respecting different levels of experience and different areas of expertise.
- Protecting private information and project-sensitive material.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Abuse of project access, maintainer trust, or security reports.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public or private channels.

Project spaces include:

- Repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews.
- Project chat, email, meetings, and documentation channels.
- Events, demos, and public communications related to the project.

## Enforcement

Report violations to the maintainer contact listed in `project-meta.yaml` under `operator.email`, or to the organization contact defined by the project.

Maintainers are responsible for reviewing reports promptly and fairly. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate this Code of Conduct.

Possible enforcement actions include:

1. Private clarification or correction.
2. Formal warning.
3. Temporary restriction from project spaces.
4. Permanent restriction from project spaces.

Maintainers must protect the privacy and safety of reporters and affected parties.

## Attribution

This policy is based on common open-source community standards and may be replaced by a project-specific policy when required by the organization, client, or publication venue.
